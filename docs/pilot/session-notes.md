## Shared-practice mental model

### Publisher

- Was it clear they were publishing selected **local** practice?
- Did explicit instruction selection feel reassuring or tedious?
- Did the local-only boundary make sense?
- Did they expect to publish a Set-up instead?
- Did they know where the resulting file should live?

Notes:

### Receiver

- Was it clear that attaching a file did not copy it into local source?
- Was the accepted snapshot/update model understandable?
- Did binding practice feel appropriately firm?
- Did adaptable defaults feel genuinely adaptable?
- Did **Leave out** feel like changing a local Set-up rather than editing organisation policy?
- Did they understand a local adaptation could remain the winning content?

## What they expected next

Do not prompt specifically for multi-source support before asking this.

- What did they expect to do next?
- Did they immediately need practice from more than one source?
- Did they expect teams/groups/accounts?
- Did they expect automatic syncing?
- Did they expect acknowledgement/compliance reporting?
- Did they expect Rack to edit the shared policy rather than their local Set-up?
